Car of the Week

This week's featured car is the 2021 Ford Mustang GT. This car is a classic American muscle car that has been around for decades. The 2021 model comes with a 5.0L V8 engine that produces 450 horsepower and 410 lb-ft of torque. It also comes with a 6-speed manual transmission or a 10-speed automatic transmission. The Mustang GT also comes with a variety of features such as a rearview camera, blind spot monitoring, and a premium sound system.
